What Aspect can and cannot tell you

Before you begin

A few things to know.

Aspect is a screening tool for autistic traits in adults. It may identify patterns worth discussing with a professional, but it cannot diagnose autism.

  1. This version is intended for adults aged 18 and over who can read English or German independently.
  2. Think about your typical experience over the last 12 months, not your best or worst day, and not what seems “most autistic.”
  3. For childhood questions, choose “I don’t remember” whenever you are unsure. You do not need to guess.
  4. Your answers are saved only in this browser on this device. You may leave items unanswered.
